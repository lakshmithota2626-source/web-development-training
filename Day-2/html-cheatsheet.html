Document Skeleton & Meta

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
    <title>Page Title</title>
    <link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css">
  </head>
  <body>
    <!-- Content goes here -->
  </body>
</html>

Note: Always specify meta viewport for responsive mobile rendering.

✍️

Text & Headings

<h1>Heading 1 (Main Page Title)</h1>
<h2>Heading 2 (Sub-section)</h2>
<h3>Heading 3 ... up to <h6></h3>

<p>Paragraph with <strong>bold text</strong> and <em>emphasis</em>.</p>
<p>Inline code: <code>const x = 10;</code></p>
<blockquote cite="url">Quoted text block</blockquote>
<span>Generic inline container</span>
<br> <!-- Line break -->
<hr> <!-- Horizontal thematic divider -->
🏗️

Semantic Structural Tags

<header>Introductory content & nav</header>
<nav>Major navigation links</nav>
<main>Dominant content of document</main>
<section>Thematic grouping of content</section>
<article>Self-contained composition</article>
<aside>Sidebar or related tangent</aside>
<footer>Copyright, author, footer links</footer>

Benefit: Improves SEO and accessibility for screen readers.

🔗

Links & Anchors

<!-- External link opening in new tab -->
<a href="https://example.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
  Visit Site
</a>

<!-- Internal page jump -->
<a href="#section-id">Jump to Section</a>

<!-- Mail and Telephone links -->
<a href="mailto:info@domain.com">Email Us</a>
<a href="tel:+1234567890">Call Us</a>

</ul>

Forms & Inputs

<form action="/submit" method="POST">
  <label for="uname">Username:</label>
  <input type="text" id="uname" name="username" required placeholder="John Doe">

  <input type="email" name="email" required placeholder="user@mail.com">
  <input type="password" name="pwd" required>
  <input type="checkbox" id="agree"> <label for="agree">Agree</label>
  <input type="radio" name="plan" value="vip"> VIP
  
  <select name="country">
    <option value="us">USA</option>
    <option value="in">India</option>
  </select>

  <textarea name="bio" rows="3"></textarea>
  <button type="submit">Submit Form</button>
</form>

Media (Images, Audio, Video, iFrames)

<!-- Responsive Image with Alt Text -->
<img src="image.jpg" alt="A descriptive label" loading="lazy" width="600" height="400">

<!-- Audio Player -->
<audio controls>
  <source src="audio.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
</audio>

<!-- Video Player -->
<video controls poster="preview.jpg" width="100%">
  <source src="movie.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

<!-- iFrame Embedded Content -->
<iframe src="https://maps.google.com" title="Map" width="100%" height="250"></iframe>
